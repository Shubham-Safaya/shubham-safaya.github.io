Short bio (40 words)

Shubham Safaya is a Senior Product Manager at Walmart Global Tech working on customer identity resolution and retail media data products. He is the author of an open-source identity resolution engine and writes the Day-N series on customer identity.

Long bio (120 words)

Shubham Safaya builds the identity layer of retail media. As a Senior Product Manager at Walmart Global Tech (Cloud & Data), he works on customer identity resolution, third-party data vendor strategy, and the audience products powering an enterprise CDP and retail media network. He holds a Master of Engineering Management from Duke University and an engineering degree from Mumbai University, with earlier product years at KPMG India and JM Family Enterprises. Outside work he maintains an open-source identity resolution engine, publishes public methodology demos of audience planning on synthetic populations, hosts the Sundays with Safaya interview series, and mentors product managers as a Top 1% Topmate mentor. He writes a daily practitioner series on customer identity, retail media, and privacy.
