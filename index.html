01 Now Building

The identity layer for the post-cookie, AI-personalized internet

Third-party cookies are dead, AI made personalization table stakes, and privacy law finally has teeth. The companies that win the next decade of advertising are the ones that solve identity — who a customer actually is across devices, stores, and sessions — with consent built in, not bolted on. That's the problem I work on every day at Walmart scale, and the body of work I'm building in public.

I / DATA IDENTITY

Resolving people, not records

Deterministic + probabilistic identity resolution across 22M+ new customer records a year. Match-rate lifts of 18%, 400K net-new IDs, and an open-source engine that shows the craft — normalization, Jaro-Winkler matching, graph clustering, survivorship.

II / ADS PERSONALIZATION

Audiences that perform

250+ audience personas powering non-endemic targeting, $15M+ incremental ad revenue, and $3.5B in GMV traced back to campaigns. Personalization that respects the person — suppression logic, consent state, and measurement designed in from day one.

III / PRIVACY BY DESIGN

Trust as infrastructure

CPRA/GDPR compliance at ingestion speed: automated purge logic, opt-out propagation, and clean-room patterns. Privacy isn't a feature you ship — it's the constraint that makes identity products durable.
